Mazedar.

It's the word that ends every good desi meal — mazedar, "full of flavor." We named the restaurant after the verdict we're chasing. Modern desi kitchen, tandoor bowls, kebab plates, chutneys made every morning. Opening soon.

ma·zay·daar — adjective. What your aunt says when the food is actually good, which is rare, which is the point.

Flavor first. Everything else second.

We're not reinventing desi food. We're building a kitchen where the three things that actually make it taste good get the budget, the prep hours, and the best bench space.

A real tandoor

Clay, charcoal, and 900 degrees. Naan that blisters in forty seconds, chicken with char you can taste before you bite.

Chutney made daily

Green, imli, garlic-chili, and one that changes with the market. Ground fresh every morning, retired every night.

Spice you can calibrate

Every plate comes at the heat you pick, one to five. Five is genuinely five. One is still mazedar — heat is a choice, flavor isn't.
